Translation of Cippus Perusinus

The Cippus Perusinus is a stone tablet (cippus) discovered on the hill of San Marco, in Perugia, Italy, in 1822. It features a long inscription engraved on a boundary stone containing legal and territorial terms.

Linguistic References: All mentioned Lithuanian, Latvian, and Russian root words can be verified via dictionary resources at ekalba.lt and Google Translate.

English Translation (Front Side)

The Ruler (or Vetulonia) saturated with justice (or court).
Ruler writes a law for you, for Etruscans.
The judge adjudicates in front of 100 Etruscans tribe. The palisade of Mainake the Punic acquire.
Here, the colony Arezzo.

English Translation (Side Inscription)

The Ruler highlights and orders to bring property pillars there.
The row of pillars is erected up to Vetulonia, up to Ilune in Tyrrhenian sea.
New sowing (or land) brings Punic diamonds and nourishes to our Ruler (or Vetulonia).
Punic Ruler looks after covenant. The rasp talking.
The bottom reached.
The conclusion.

Historical Conclusion

It seems that Cippus Perusinus is a Peace Treaty between the Etruscans and Phoenicians.
